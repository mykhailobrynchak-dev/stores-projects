  • ANRI-PHARM: average distance fell 3.63 → 2.56 km. The 0–2 km share rose from 32% to 43%, while the long 4 km+ tail shrank from 38% to 17% of deliveries.

Takeaways

  • Radius customization improved unit economics — lower CPO, shorter distances and better CP Margin for both networks; TAISTRA crossed into a positive margin.
  • Distances compressed — the 4 km+ tail shrank sharply, which is what drove CPO down.
  • ANRI quality jumped (Availability 76→94%, Bad orders 30→14%, Honey 43→55%) alongside the radius work.
  • Watch-outs: TAISTRA Ternopil lost CP Margin (small sample, widened radius) and TAISTRA Availability dipped (87→81%) — review locally. ANRI is still slightly margin-negative overall.
